Two seats owners hire first

Most small firms feel the strain in one of two places: the open files, or the new calls. Hire for the one that is actually slowing you down. A later search can cover the other.

Role

Bilingual virtual paralegal

Supports matters that are already open. They keep the file in order, prepare routine drafts for your review, track deadlines, request records, and update clients in English or Spanish with the status you have approved. A paralegal works under attorney supervision at your firm. You keep the legal judgment.

Role

Bilingual intake coordinator

Handles the new lead. They answer the call, including callers who want to speak Spanish, ask the screening questions you wrote, enter the person in the system you already use, and book the consult. Open this seat when new callers need Spanish and the files are already under control.

Questions from firm owners

Straight answers about the two roles, the hours, and what hiring with OYT actually involves.

Who does this person work for?

They work for your firm. Outsource Your Tasks sources, screens, and introduces candidates. You hire directly, you set the work, and you manage the day. The person is dedicated to you.

What is the difference between the paralegal and the intake coordinator?

A paralegal supports open matters: the file, drafts for your review, deadlines, records, and client updates you have approved. A paralegal works under attorney supervision. An intake coordinator handles the new lead: the first call, the questions you wrote, the entry in your system, and the booked consult.

Can they speak with Spanish-speaking clients?

That is the reason to hire a bilingual person for this seat. You still decide what they may say to a client. Spanish is the language of the conversation. Your attorneys keep the legal judgment.

Will their hours overlap my office?

For these roles, OYT looks to Latin America because the region shares the business day with US firms. The time zone is part of what you agree before anyone starts.

How are candidates vetted?

Each search is run fresh for your role. OYT checks that the experience fits, evaluates English, and confirms the computer, internet, and backup are ready for remote work. Rate and time zone are presented up front. Finalists are interviewed by OYT. You receive a resume, a DISC profile, and a recorded video interview before you meet them.

How long does hiring take, and what does it cost?

Most searches finish in a few weeks. The timing depends on the role. OYT charges a one-time placement fee and does not add an ongoing markup to the person's pay. The fee for a search is on the pricing page, and the strategy call is where you confirm it fits the seat you described.

Tell us which seat you need

Bring a rough task list, or just the problem. Missed Spanish-speaking calls, a deadline calendar only you understand, or files that never quite get opened. The strategy call is complimentary.
