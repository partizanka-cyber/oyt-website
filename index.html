A better way to hire

We combine global reach, hands-on recruiting, and direct-hire placement to give you more control, lower costs, and better support throughout the hiring process.

We know where to find qualified nearshore and global talent. We source across the Americas, Europe, Asia, and Africa, including U.S.-based talent for senior and fractional roles, helping you reduce labor costs by up to 70%.

Traditional recruiters often charge around 20% of first-year salary, which is $20,000 on a $100,000 role. OYT charges one fixed placement fee instead.

No AI filters, ATS keyword matching, or resume roulette. A real person reviews and interviews every candidate before you ever meet them.

Your new hire works directly for you from day one. There is no middleman, no agency between you and your team, and no ongoing markup.

If the placement does not work out within your selected guarantee period, we source a replacement at no additional placement fee (see Pricing for coverage options).

Practical tools and video guides help you set expectations, establish workflows, and integrate your new hire successfully from day one.
