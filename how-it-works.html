The six steps

01 Strategy call 02 Global search 03 Shortlist review 04 Interviews 05 Direct hire 06 Onboarding guide
01

You book a complimentary strategy call

A working session focused on role mapping. We map your team structure, uncover the bottlenecks, and define the exact role that will move the needle most. You walk away with clarity before we ever start searching.

  • Team structure review
  • Role definition
  • Salary benchmarks
02

We manage the full search

We write the job description, pay for the ads, and search the full global market from scratch. Most agencies pull from a database they already have. We go out fresh every time, prequalify, and personally interview the top candidates. You do none of this.

  • Job description
  • Ad spend covered
  • OYT Vetting
  • Recorded interviews
03

You review the shortlist

We send you a shortlist of candidates with their resumes, DISC profiles, and recorded video interviews. You review on your own time and choose your favorites.

  • Resumes
  • DISC profiles
  • Video interviews
04

We schedule the interviews

We coordinate the interviews between you and your top picks so you can meet them, ask your questions, and make a confident final decision.

  • Calendar coordination
  • Time zone alignment
  • Interview prep notes
05

You hire them directly

Onboard them directly or through a third-party payroll provider. Either way, they work exclusively for you and become a dedicated part of your team.

  • No middleman
  • No ongoing fees
  • Exclusive to you
06

We hand you the onboarding guide

A full onboarding and management guide so you know exactly how to set them up, integrate them into your team, and keep them long term.

  • Onboarding & management videos
  • Sample contract
  • Payment methods
