Our Screening Process.

  • We source > 100 candidates for each role.
  • Interview 20-25 applicants for each role.
  • Evaluate English fluency and relevant job experience.
  • Assess if candidates' skills match a job description.
  • Evaluate computer specs, internet speed, and validate backup.
  • Assess and negotiate competitive market rate based on the role and experience.
  • Provide top candidates (with resumes, DISC profiles, and video interviews).
